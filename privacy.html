Autoclips Upload – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Controller

Thade, Aachen, Germany. Contact: github.com/ttraennnapp/autoclips-legal/issues

2. What data is processed

When the operator signs in with TikTok, the app receives from TikTok: an access token and refresh token, the account's open ID, basic profile information (display name, avatar) and, for statistics, the list of the account's own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

3. Purpose

The data is used only to upload videos as drafts to the operator's own TikTok account and to show the operator how those videos perform. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

All data is stored locally on the operator's own computer. Nothing is stored on servers of the app, because the app has no server. Tokens are never shared or published.

5. Sharing

Data is not sold or shared with third parties. Video files are sent only to TikTok through its official API.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the operator signs out or revokes access in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); then they are deleted. Statistics are kept as long as the operator uses the app.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at the address above.
