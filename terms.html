Autoclips Upload – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. What the app is

Autoclips Upload is a private desktop tool operated by Thade (Aachen, Germany). It turns Twitch highlight clips into vertical videos and uploads them as drafts to the operator's own TikTok account, where they are reviewed and posted manually. The app is not offered to the public.

2. Who may use it

The app is used only by its operator. Signing in with TikTok is limited to the operator's own TikTok account. Anyone else who signs in does so at their own risk and receives no service.

3. Content and rights

Only clips from streamers who have allowed their clips to be used are processed. The operator is responsible for every video uploaded through the app and for compliance with the TikTok Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Rights holders can request removal of a video at any time via the contact below.

4. TikTok integration

The app uses the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API. It never posts publicly without the operator's explicit action and never stores your TikTok password.
